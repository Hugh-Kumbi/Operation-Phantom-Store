# Repository Disclaimers

## Detection Rules
These rules were developed from infrastructure and behavioural observations documented during the investigation. They are intended as examples for threat hunting and defensive engineering. Organizations should validate, tune, and adapt these rules to their own environments before production deployment.

## Threat Intelligence Exports (STIX 2.1 / MISP)
The STIX 2.1 and MISP exports included in this repository were manually authored from intelligence gathered during the investigation to demonstrate familiarity with structured threat intelligence standards. They are intended for educational and portfolio purposes rather than as production-ready threat feeds.

## General Notes
- No guarantee of completeness or accuracy is provided
- These artifacts reflect a specific point-in-time investigation
- Threat actors evolve—indicators may expire or change context
- Always correlate with additional sources before acting
