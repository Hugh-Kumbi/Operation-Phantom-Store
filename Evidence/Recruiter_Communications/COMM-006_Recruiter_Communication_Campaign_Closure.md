## Campaign Assessment

The investigation documented a complete operational lifecycle consisting of:

1. Initial recruiter contact.
2. Structured onboarding.
3. Progressive trust-building.

5. Operational task execution.

This sequence demonstrates a repeatable and structured methodology rather than isolated or opportunistic interactions.

The investigation highlighted several important defensive considerations:

- Passive OSINT techniques can produce substantial intelligence without requiring intrusive activity.
- Structured documentation improves evidence correlation and analytical confidence.
