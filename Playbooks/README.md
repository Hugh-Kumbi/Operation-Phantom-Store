# Incident Response Playbooks

## Overview

This directory contains operational guidance for responding to infrastructure associated with Operation Phantom Store.

Unlike the analytical documents contained elsewhere in this repository, these playbooks focus on defensive operations and incident response.

The procedures are designed for SOC analysts, DFIR teams, and incident responders who encounter campaign indicators within their environment.

### Incident Response Playbook

End-to-end incident handling process.

### Threat Hunting Guide

Recommended hunting hypotheses and queries.

### IOC Response Checklist

Quick-reference checklist for triage and containment.

### SOC Runbook

Operational workflow for Tier 1–3 analysts.

### Executive Incident Brief

Management-level summary and recommended actions.
