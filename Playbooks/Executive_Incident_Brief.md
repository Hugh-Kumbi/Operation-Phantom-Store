# Executive Incident Brief

## Summary

Operation Phantom Store is a recruiter-led campaign using multiple web domains and shared backend infrastructure to support fraudulent activity.

The campaign demonstrates rapid infrastructure rotation, modern cloud hosting, and consistent technical characteristics across operational portals.

## Business Risk

- Potential financial fraud
- Exposure of user credentials
- Reputational damage
- Loss of employee trust
- Increased incident response costs

## Current Assessment

Confidence in the infrastructure relationships is **High** based on multiple independent technical and behavioural indicators.

## Recommended Actions

- Block identified infrastructure.
- Monitor for new related domains.
- Increase user awareness regarding unsolicited recruitment offers.
- Maintain updated IOC lists and detection content.
- Share indicators with internal security teams and trusted partners, as appropriate.
